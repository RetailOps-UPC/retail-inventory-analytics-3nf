## How to read this report

- **Acceptance rate** is the share of extracted rows that passed every ERROR-severity rule. It is a cleanliness measure of the *source*, not of the warehouse: quarantined rows never reach the warehouse at all.
- **Ground truth exists only for synthetic data.** Recall here is measured against the defects the generator planted. It says nothing about defects nobody thought to plant; against real data there is no list to score against.
- **Severity decides the reaction.** FATAL aborts the run, ERROR quarantines the offending rows, WARNING is reported and the rows still load.
